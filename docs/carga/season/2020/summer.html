During their internships, they learn about the ocean and actively participate in marine life rescue efforts. However, a mysterious group calling themselves the "Iron Eagle Squadron" suddenly descends upon Yueyou Island, using a variety of high-tech equipment to capture a variety of marine life. Dr. Huanzhong, a master of machine manufacturing, is behind this operation. Xiya, Haixin, and Xiafan then wield their magical powers to engage in a battle to protect marine life.
